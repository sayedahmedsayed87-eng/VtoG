Data & account
Clear app cache

Removes the folder lists, usage numbers, sign-in shortcut and remembered folder stored on this device. The app reloads them from the server. Nothing in Drive or in your account changes.

Clear download history

Deletes the records of your finished and failed tasks from our servers. Tasks still waiting are not touched, and your usage totals stay the same.

Deactivate account

Cancels every task that is still waiting and removes your saved Drive folders from the app. You can come back any time by reactivating.

⚠️

Remove this folder?

This only removes it from the app — it won't delete the folder from Drive.

⚠️

Clear local cache?

Here's what will happen:

  • Saved folder lists on this device are deleted.
  • Your remembered destination folder is forgotten.
  • The next time you open the app, it has to load everything again, so it may feel slower for a moment.

Nothing in Google Drive is deleted, and your downloads are not affected.

⚠️

Clear download history?

Here's what will happen:

  • The records of your finished and failed tasks (all task types) are deleted from our servers.
  • This can't be undone.

Tasks that are still waiting are not touched. Files already in Google Drive stay where they are, and your usage totals and quota don't change.

⚠️

Deactivate your account?

Here's what will happen:

  • Every task of yours that is still waiting (all task types) is cancelled and removed. It will not be restored.
  • Your saved Google Drive folders are removed from the app. You will need to add them again.
  • You can't use Clip Sense until you reactivate. Opening the app shows a Reactivate button.
  • A task that is already being processed may still finish.

Nothing in Google Drive is deleted. Your usage totals are kept, and your quota period keeps running while you're away, so reactivating doesn't reset it. To stop this app from accessing a folder, also remove its sharing in Google Drive.
